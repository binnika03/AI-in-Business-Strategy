# Artificial Intelligence in Business Strategy
## How AI-Driven Analytics Is Reshaping Decision-Making in Business

MBA Capstone Project | JG University, Ahmedabad | April 2026  
Guided by Dr. Pratik Darji | Sponsored by ASIA Charitable Trust

---

## 🎯 Objective
To study how AI-driven analytics reshapes strategic decision-making across 
Infrastructure, Retail, Footwear, and Textile sectors in Ahmedabad.

## 🔬 Research Methodology
- Research Design: Descriptive
- Sample Size: 629 respondents
- Sampling Method: Convenience Sampling
- Data Collection: Structured questionnaire via Google Forms
- Tools: SPSS and Microsoft Excel

## 📊 Statistical Tests Applied
- Percentage Analysis & Rating Scale Analysis
- One-Way ANOVA (p = 0.000 — significant difference in AI effectiveness across sectors)
- Chi-Square Test (p < 0.05 — significant association between employee role and AI perception)

## 💡 Key Findings
- 85.7% of respondents are aware of AI tools in strategic decision-making
- 68.7% agree AI is integrated into long-term business strategy
- AI has the highest impact on Demand Forecasting (1.76) and Inventory Management (1.97)
- 70.1% confirmed AI reduced operational costs in their organization
- Top barriers: Data security concerns (29.8%), Lack of skilled workforce (18.2%)
